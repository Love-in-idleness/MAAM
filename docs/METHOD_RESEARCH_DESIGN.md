**一、数学史提供的设计约束 / 1. Design constraints informed by mathematical history**

| 中文 | English |
| --- | --- |
| 以下是对原始论文或译文的局部阅读，以及据此作出的工程推断，不是数学史的完整因果解释。成功案例有幸存者偏差，不能据此预言新抽象必有价值。 | These are selected readings of original works or translations, followed by engineering inferences—not a complete causal history. Successful examples have survivor bias and cannot guarantee the value of a new abstraction. |
| **Galois：改变问题的表达与可用对象。** 原文明确讨论添入已知量如何改变方程的性质与困难，并用置换组织可解性问题。工程推断：记录背景变化、对象之间的对应和问题转换；只比较最终公式长度会遗漏这种变化。[原文英译，Principles 与后续命题](https://mathcircle.berkeley.edu/sites/default/files/handouts/2022/Galois_0.pdf#page=11)。 | **Galois: change the representation and available objects.** The memoir discusses how adjoining known quantities changes equations and organizes solvability through substitutions. Engineering inference: record background changes, object correspondences, and problem transformations; final formula length alone misses them. [Translated memoir, Principles onward](https://mathcircle.berkeley.edu/sites/default/files/handouts/2022/Galois_0.pdf#page=11). |
| **Noether：抽取条件，让一组论证覆盖多类对象。** 理想论原文从分解问题走向一般环及有限性条件，并讨论多项式、数论和模的实例。工程推断：一般定理、必要假设与已核验实例共同构成工具；未经证明地删除假设或仅复制实例不算推广。[原文英译，导言与目录](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1401.2577)。 | **Noether: isolate conditions that support arguments across objects.** The paper develops decomposition through general rings and finiteness conditions, with polynomial, arithmetic, and module cases. Engineering inference: a tool includes the general result, its assumptions, and checked instances. Dropping assumptions without proof or merely duplicating instances does not establish generalization. [Translation, introduction and contents](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1401.2577). |
| **Riemann：新对象可以改变可提出的问题。** 讲演区分多重广延量及其度量关系。工程推断：允许新规格、新实例和新任务进入候选档案；旧题上的压缩不能独占全部评价。[讲演英译，Plan 与第一部分](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Extras/Riemann_geometry/)。 | **Riemann: new objects can change which questions can be posed.** The lecture separates multiply extended magnitudes from their metric relations. Engineering inference: admit new specifications, instances, and tasks to the candidate archive; compression on old tasks cannot exhaust evaluation. [Translated lecture, opening and Part I](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Extras/Riemann_geometry/). |
| **Grothendieck：统一接口需要实际的存在性与适用条件。** Tôhoku 论文把层上同调和模的导出函子纳入共同框架，并研究何时有足够的内射或投射对象。工程推断：抽象接口、实现证书和适用条件一起保存，不能只保存一个更短的名称。[原文导言](https://matematicas.unex.es/~navarro/res/tohoku.pdf)。 | **Grothendieck: common interfaces need existence and applicability conditions.** The Tôhoku paper develops a shared setting for sheaf cohomology and derived functors of modules, including conditions for enough injectives or projectives. Engineering inference: retain interfaces, realization certificates, and hypotheses together. [Original introduction](https://matematicas.unex.es/~navarro/res/tohoku.pdf). |
| **Langlands：连接也可以先作为研究纲领存在。** 致 Weil 的信把若干 L 级数放入更一般的问题中。工程推断：把猜想中的对应、已证明的桥接和已观察用途区分开；允许保存待发展的连接，不把潜在无限用途记成现成收益。[1967 年原信](https://publications.ias.edu/sites/default/files/letter-to-weil-1967-01_rpl_5.pdf)。 | **Langlands: connections may initially be a research program.** The letter to Weil places several L-series within broader questions. Engineering inference: distinguish conjectured correspondences, proved bridges, and observed applications. Preserve prospective connections without booking infinitely many hypothetical uses as gains. [Original 1967 letter](https://publications.ias.edu/sites/default/files/letter-to-weil-1967-01_rpl_5.pdf). |
| **Hilbert 与 Gödel：问题、证明系统和限度都应明确。** Hilbert 的问题表体现问题选择的作用；Gödel 的命题 VI 在其明确的有效性与一致性条件下给出不可判定命题。工程推断：固定基础并保留“未知”；核验成功不回答重要性，搜索失败也不证明不可能。后两句是评价设计原则，并非从不完备性直接推出。[Hilbert 原讲演英译](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/71655/pg71655-images.html)，[Gödel 原文英译，命题 VI](https://gtts.ehu.es/German/Docencia/2021/MTBYC/pdf/Godel.pdf#page=60)。 | **Hilbert and Gödel: specify problems, proof systems, and limits.** Hilbert's problem list foregrounds problem selection; Gödel's Proposition VI establishes undecidability under its stated effectiveness and consistency conditions. Engineering inference: fix foundations and preserve unknowns. Verification does not decide importance, and unsuccessful search does not establish impossibility. Those are evaluation principles, not direct consequences of incompleteness. [Hilbert](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/71655/pg71655-images.html), [Gödel, Proposition VI](https://gtts.ehu.es/German/Docencia/2021/MTBYC/pdf/Godel.pdf#page=60). |

**三、对象、工具包与最小表示 / 3. Objects, packages, and the minimum representation**

| 中文 | English |
| --- | --- |
| **定义与规格：**定义给出对象或运算的表达；规格给出所需结构和条件。一个规格可有多个实现。**命题：**带类型、参数、假设与结论的断言。**证明：**该断言的一份证书，可有多个替代证书。 | **Definitions and specifications:** definitions express objects or operations; specifications state required structure and conditions, potentially with several realizations. **Propositions:** typed assertions with parameters, assumptions, and conclusions. **Proofs:** certificates, with alternative certificates permitted. |
| **还要保存的内容：**实例和反例、算法及计算行为、结构之间的映射与运输、未解决问题、猜想、搜索与失败记录。存在性证书不自动提供可执行算法；方法名称也不自动提供一般过程。 | **Additional content:** instances and counterexamples, algorithms and computational behavior, structure maps and transport, open problems, conjectures, and search/failure records. Existence does not automatically provide an executable algorithm, and a method name does not provide a general procedure. |
| **方法的操作性定义：**在给定背景下，帮助选择、构造或组合已有步骤的一份可复用说明或程序。它可以是不完备的启发式；每个最终数学结论仍须独立核验。 | **Operational definition of a method:** reusable guidance or a procedure for selecting, constructing, or combining steps in a fixed background. It may be an incomplete heuristic; each resulting mathematical conclusion still requires verification. |
| **表示顺序：**能写成引理就先写引理；需要产出对象就写构造；真正需要选择顺序或搜索时再加简短指南；出现实际重复执行需求后才考虑 tactic 或脚本。当前不建通用方法语言、知识图数据库或新的证明内核。 | **Representation order:** use a lemma when sufficient, a construction when an object is produced, and short guidance when selection or search matters. Add a tactic or script only for demonstrated execution needs. No general method language, graph database, or new proof kernel is planned now. |
| **工具包分为三部分：**数学源码及完整依赖；给 AI 使用的接口说明和必要执行代码；独立的证据清单。第三部分保存来源、版本、目标身份、核验结果、用量与失败，不能把未证猜想混入可调用的定理库。 | **Three package parts:** mathematical source with complete dependencies; guidance and necessary execution code; and a separate evidence manifest. The manifest records provenance, versions, goal identity, verification, usage, and failures. Unproved conjectures never enter the callable theorem library as facts. |
| 首版用现有文件和 JSON 记录即可。指南只需说明：输入及类型、可检查前提、参数选择、步骤或辅助构造、输出和剩余义务、直接适用边界。来源和具体应用记录留在证据清单，测量任务的人工答案不得混入通用指南。 | Existing files and JSON records suffice initially. Guidance specifies inputs/types, checkable premises, parameter choices, steps or auxiliary constructions, outputs/residual obligations, and direct applicability boundaries. Provenance and application records remain in the evidence manifest; target answers stay out of general guidance. |
| 核验状态、应用证据与新颖性分开。人工应用、模型独立应用、冻结后的后续应用不能互相冒充；新颖性默认未知或已知工具重组，不把“没有搜到出处”写成首创。 | Verification, application evidence, and novelty are separate. Author applications, independent model applications, and later frozen evaluations are distinct. Novelty defaults to unknown or recombination of known tools; failure to find a precedent is not priority evidence. |

**四、用户提出的边界案例 / 4. Required mathematical cases**

| 中文 | English |
| --- | --- |
| **实数：**保留构造 C；新增规格 A、证书 A(C)、由该实例导出的存在性和需要时的唯一性/同构。存在性与实现放在包的实例部分，依赖指向真实构造。一般条件定理不必依赖某个实例的存在性；按实际依赖计量。“完备”须指明意义与结构相容性。 | **Reals:** retain construction C; add specification A, certificate A(C), witnessed existence, and any required uniqueness/isomorphism. Realization evidence belongs with instances and retains the construction dependency. Conditional general theorems need not depend on a particular existence witness. Specify completeness and compatibility precisely. |
| **行列式：**保留莱布尼兹公式作为实现和计算表达；证明其满足交替、多线性、归一化规格及所需唯一性。交换变号在特征二时不能替代交替性；不能把“存在一个行列式”当作已有高效求值算法。 | **Determinant:** retain the Leibniz formula as an implementation and computational expression; prove alternation, multilinearity, normalization, and required uniqueness. Skew symmetry alone does not replace alternation in characteristic two. Existence does not establish an efficient evaluation algorithm. |
| **Cayley–Hamilton 与推广：**交换环版本作为新节点，保留域上版本，以可核验的特化连接。新增范围用明确的额外实例及适用条件说明；不能按潜在无穷多实例直接计收益。 | **Cayley–Hamilton and generalization:** retain the field version and add the commutative-ring version with checked specialization. Demonstrate additional scope through explicit cases and hypotheses, without counting infinitely many hypothetical instances as benefits. |
| **1000 与 10×120：**保留短证书与较长的模块化工具表示。前者可能更适合当前证书选择，后者可能改善调用或组合；后续用途和共享内容必须实际计量。**200 与 100：**不设线性、平方或指数难度公式，直接观察固定背景和预算下的使用结果。 | **1000 versus 10×120:** preserve the short certificate alongside the longer modular representation. They may serve different purposes; measure actual reuse and sharing. **200 versus 100:** impose no linear, quadratic, or exponential difficulty law. Observe use under fixed background and resources. |
| 上述案例的细节沿用[分层框架](COMPRESSION_FRAMEWORK.md)，本阶段只作为语义验收案例，不要求把实数、行列式或整个交换环理论重新形式化。 | Detailed cases remain in the [layered framework](COMPRESSION_FRAMEWORK.md). They are semantic acceptance cases, not a requirement to formalize the reals, determinants, or commutative algebra anew in this phase. |

**五、采用的计量与保留规则 / 5. Measurements and retention rules**

| 中文 | English |
| --- | --- |
| **先固定比较条件。** 每次记录基础、公理、Lean 版本、共享库、目标及其身份、可用工具、模型设置和资源预算。已有广泛数学背景不重复收费；模型内部实际掌握了什么仍未知。限制导入不能消除预训练知识。 | **Fix the comparison context first.** Record foundations, axioms, Lean version, shared library, goal identities, tools, model settings, and budgets. Do not recharge broad prior mathematical knowledge. Internal competence remains unknown; restricted imports do not erase pretraining. |
| **正确性是门槛。** 目标不变、公理与依赖合规、Lean 核验通过，才能进入已验证材料。定义替换另需实现或运输证书；推广另需正确的特化关系。通过核验本身不证明重要性、原创性或一般适用性。 | **Correctness is a gate.** Verified material must preserve the goal and pass Lean with allowed axioms and dependencies. Replacing a definition additionally needs realization or transport evidence; generalization needs a correct specialization relation. Verification alone establishes neither importance, novelty, nor general usefulness. |
| **原证选择规则不变。** 在完全相同目标上，只有核验通过且完整 Proof 源码 token 严格减少的候选才替换原证。等长、更长和失败材料留作记录。较长的模块化证明或指南可以进入候选档案，不自动进入默认部署包。 | **Keep the existing proof-selection rule.** Replace a certificate only with a verified candidate for the same goal whose complete Proof source has strictly fewer source tokens. Retain equal, longer, and failed attempts as records. Longer modular proofs or guides may enter the candidate archive without automatically entering the deployed package. |
| **整包压缩：**固定文件集合、路径、顺序和现有规范编码，主指标用 zlib level 9，bzip2 作敏感性检查。若两个部署材料集合为 D_A、D_B，报告 ΔC = C(encode(D_A)) − C(encode(D_B))。新包、桥接、指南和专用执行器均计一次；不能把各文件单独压缩后相加。 | **Package compression:** freeze the file set, paths, ordering, and existing canonical encoding. Use zlib level 9 as the primary measure, with bzip2 as a sensitivity check. For deployment sets D_A and D_B, report ΔC = C(encode(D_A)) − C(encode(D_B)). Count new packages, bridges, guidance, and dedicated execution code once, compressing the set jointly. |
| 分别报告非共享部署材料、包含明确共享源码 K 的本地整体档案。前者不是条件 Kolmogorov 复杂度，后者也不等于非共享材料压缩值加 C(K)。共同实验框架的准备成本单列一次；原始日志和研究证据另存，不能删失败日志来缩小“证明包”。 | Report nonshared deployment material and the joint local archive including explicitly shared source K separately. Neither is conditional Kolmogorov complexity, and joint compression is not the sum of separately compressed parts. Report shared experimental infrastructure once; preserve evidence and failure logs outside the deployment measurement. |
| **覆盖必须相同。** 每个目标以冻结参考证书作为回退，再按原证规则选择候选。没有经过核验的回退时，报告缺失覆盖，不给全体目标的压缩优劣结论。参考证书供离线核验与计量，不进入模型输入；指南首次试验的主要指标是求解表现，压缩只作附带计量。 | **Match coverage.** Each goal has a frozen verified fallback, with candidate selection following the retention rule. Missing fallbacks mean incomplete coverage and no full-portfolio compression ranking. References serve offline verification and measurement, never model input. The guidance pilot primarily measures solving; compression is secondary. |
| **变化传播按真实依赖计算。** 新定义、存在性或桥接证书加入依赖图后，相关闭包内的新增源码只计一次；反向依赖仅列出可能受影响的结果，不触发全库重证。若重述了目标，先以显式运输回到原目标再比较；无法建立这种对应时作为新目标另记，不宣称同目标压缩。 | **Propagate changes through actual dependencies.** Charge newly required source once within the relevant dependency closure. Reverse dependencies identify potentially affected results without triggering library-wide reproving. Transport reformulated conclusions explicitly back to the original goal before comparison; otherwise record new goals without a same-goal compression claim. |
| **实际使用：**逐任务记录首轮成功、预算内成功、完整调用数、输入/输出/推理用量、核验成本、时间及错误类别。失败也占资源，未知量不填零。指南存储一次，但每次进入提示的输入成本重复计入。准备源码与已知人工时间另记，历史准备时间未知就保留未知。 | **Operational use:** record first-attempt and within-budget success, calls, input/output/reasoning usage, verification costs, elapsed time, and failure categories per task. Failures consume resources; unknowns remain unknown. Store guidance once but charge each prompt occurrence. Track preparation source and known author time separately, retaining unknown historical effort. |
| **适用范围：**用实际的结构、实例、已核验应用及反例说明，不以文件数或潜在无穷实例计收益。**方法使用：**显式引理依赖或核验后的实例应用可作证据；模型自述不够。内联证明未匹配时可记未分类，当前不建设一般语义识别器。 | **Scope:** record actual structures, instances, checked applications, and counterexamples, rather than file counts or infinitely many possible cases. **Method use:** explicit lemma dependencies or checked instantiations provide evidence; self-report does not. Unmatched inline arguments may remain unclassified; no general semantic recognizer is required now. |
