### 推导规则 / Implication rules

- `rh-prime-count` · 黎曼猜想 / Riemann hypothesis → 素数计数的平方根误差界 / Square-root error in prime counting. The prime-counting error estimate is the forward direction of the classical equivalence. [Bombieri, pp. 110–111](<https://www.claymath.org/library/monographs/MPPc.pdf>)
- `prime-count-rh` · 素数计数的平方根误差界 / Square-root error in prime counting → 黎曼猜想 / Riemann hypothesis. The converse direction of the same classical equivalence. [Bombieri, pp. 110–111](<https://www.claymath.org/library/monographs/MPPc.pdf>)
- `rh-short-gaps` · 黎曼猜想 / Riemann hypothesis → 相邻素数间隙的平方根级上界 / Square-root scale prime gaps. Cramér’s conditional bound is stated explicitly as (1.11). [Carneiro–Milinovich–Soundararajan §1.2, (1.11)](<https://doi.org/10.4171/CMH/467>)
- `rh-lindelof` · 黎曼猜想 / Riemann hypothesis → 林德勒夫猜想 / Lindelöf hypothesis. Section 13.1 states that RH implies the critical-line subpower bound for ζ. [Titchmarsh §13.1](<https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~zeilberg/EM18/TitchmarshZeta.pdf>)
- `lindelof-moments` · 林德勒夫猜想 / Lindelöf hypothesis → ζ 函数所有正整数阶矩的界 / All positive integral moments of ζ. Pointwise Lindelöf bounds with exponent ε/k give the moment bounds. [Titchmarsh §13.2, Theorem 13.2](<https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~zeilberg/EM18/TitchmarshZeta.pdf>)
- `moments-lindelof` · ζ 函数所有正整数阶矩的界 / All positive integral moments of ζ → 林德勒夫猜想 / Lindelöf hypothesis. The converse is included in the stated equivalences. [Titchmarsh §13.2, Theorem 13.2](<https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~zeilberg/EM18/TitchmarshZeta.pdf>)
- `p-eq-np-ph` · P = NP → 多项式层级坍缩到 P / PH collapses to P. If P equals NP, the polynomial hierarchy collapses to P. [Cook §5, Proposition 2 discussion](<https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/pvsnp.pdf>)
- `ph-p-eq-np` · 多项式层级坍缩到 P / PH collapses to P → P = NP. The first existential level of PH includes NP. [Cook §5, Proposition 2 discussion](<https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/pvsnp.pdf>)
- `p-eq-np-np-conp` · P = NP → NP = coNP. Since deterministic polynomial-time decision is closed under complement, NP would be too. [Cook §1, Definitions 3–4](<https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/pvsnp.pdf>)
- `p-eq-np-factoring` · P = NP → 整数分解有经典确定性多项式时间算法 / Deterministic polynomial-time factoring. The existence of a proper divisor below a binary threshold is in NP. Binary search finds the least proper divisor, and repeating on the reduced quotient produces its prime factors. [Cook §§1–2, the language L_fact](<https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/pvsnp.pdf>)
- `p-eq-np-gi` · P = NP → 图同构可在确定性多项式时间判定 / Graph isomorphism in P. A bijection of the vertices that preserves adjacency gives a polynomially checkable certificate, so GI is in NP. [Babai §1.1, GI definition](<https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03547>)
- `p-ne-np-pspace` · P ≠ NP → P ≠ PSPACE. The chain P ⊆ NP ⊆ PSPACE makes the outer inclusion strict. [Cook §5, complexity-class inclusion chain](<https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/pvsnp.pdf>)
- `automorphy-entireness` · Langlands 互反律（Artin 表示情形） / Langlands reciprocity for Artin representations → Artin L 函数整性 / Artin holomorphy. The cuspidal standard L-function transfers holomorphy to the matching Artin L-function. Nontriviality excludes the trivial character pole. [Arthur §26, (26.6) and discussion](<https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/cmip04c.pdf>)
- `bsd-tunnell` · BSD 猜想（秩形式） / Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer rank conjecture → Tunnell 计数判据的逆向 / Converse of Tunnell’s criterion. Rank BSD makes vanishing of the central L-value equivalent to positive rational rank, supplying the missing direction of Tunnell’s criterion. [Wiles, pp. 38–39](<https://www.claymath.org/library/monographs/MPPc.pdf>)
- `hodge-lefschetz` · 有理 Hodge 猜想 / Rational Hodge conjecture → 复数域上的 Lefschetz 标准猜想 B / Lefschetz standard conjecture B over C. The inverse Lefschetz correspondence has a rational Hodge class on X × X by Künneth and Poincaré duality. Hodge for that product makes it algebraic. [Voisin §3.2, Conjecture 2](<https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~claire.voisin/Articlesweb/voisinhodge.pdf>)
- `hodge-absolute` · 有理 Hodge 猜想 / Rational Hodge conjecture → Hodge 类皆为绝对 Hodge 类 / All Hodge classes are absolute Hodge. Algebraic cycle classes are absolute Hodge, so the Hodge conjecture implies that all rational Hodge classes are absolute. [Voisin §4.3, Proposition 2 and Conjecture 7](<https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~claire.voisin/Articlesweb/voisinhodge.pdf>)
- `hodge-tate-abelian` · 有理 Hodge 猜想 / Rational Hodge conjecture → 有限域上阿贝尔簇的完全 Tate 猜想 / Full Tate conjecture for abelian varieties over finite fields. The proof cites the reduction from the complex Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties to Tate for abelian varieties in characteristic p and uses semisimplicity for the full form. [Milne Theorem 3.2 proof](<https://jmilne.org/math/articles/LFF.pdf>)
- `hodge-positivity` · 有理 Hodge 猜想 / Rational Hodge conjecture → 正特征阿贝尔簇的 Hodge 型标准猜想 / Hodge standard conjecture for abelian varieties in positive characteristic. The proof cites the implication from the Hodge conjecture for complex CM abelian varieties established by Milne (2002). [Milne Theorem 3.3 proof](<https://jmilne.org/math/articles/LFF.pdf>)
- `finite-lefschetz-tate` · Lefschetz 标准猜想 B（有限域代数闭包） / Lefschetz standard conjecture B over finite-field closures → 有限域上阿贝尔簇的完全 Tate 猜想 / Full Tate conjecture for abelian varieties over finite fields. Apply the theorem separately at each prime. [Milne Theorem 2(a) and Theorem 3.2](<https://jmilne.org/math/articles/LFF.pdf>)
- `finite-lefschetz-positivity` · Lefschetz 标准猜想 B（有限域代数闭包） / Lefschetz standard conjecture B over finite-field closures → 正特征阿贝尔簇的 Hodge 型标准猜想 / Hodge standard conjecture for abelian varieties in positive characteristic. Apply the theorem separately at each prime. [Milne Theorem 2(b) and Theorem 3.3](<https://jmilne.org/math/articles/LFF.pdf>)
- `finite-lefschetz-almost-algebraic` · Lefschetz 标准猜想 B（有限域代数闭包） / Lefschetz standard conjecture B over finite-field closures → 阿贝尔簇的 Hodge 类近似代数 / Almost-algebraicity of Hodge classes on abelian varieties. The theorem turns finite-field Lefschetz correspondences into specialisation data for absolute Hodge classes. [Milne Theorem 1.4](<https://jmilne.org/math/articles/LFF.pdf>)
- `binary-ternary` · 强哥德巴赫猜想 / Binary Goldbach conjecture → 三素数定理 / Ternary Goldbach theorem. For odd n > 5, subtract 3 and apply binary Goldbach. The target already has an unconditional proof by Helfgott. [Helfgott §1 and Main Theorem](<https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.7748>)
